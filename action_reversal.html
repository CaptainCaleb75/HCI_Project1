Action Reversal

Mistakes happen, so actions should be easy to take back. Add a few tasks, delete one, and use Undo to bring it back right where it was.

    No tasks yet. Type one above and select Add task.

    What to notice: Deleting may not ask for confirmation. It just happens, and a message appears with an Undo button for a few seconds. Undo puts the task back in its original position.
